# HorizonXI Policy Review — 2026-08-19

## Official Sources Reviewed

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| https://horizonxi.com/addons | Only addons listed on the approved lists may be used. Custom addons must be publicly hosted and submitted for review; unlisted addons should be considered prohibited. |
| https://horizonxi.com/rules | Non-approved addons and third-party tools are strictly prohibited. The rules state that actions which do not originate from player inputs are not allowed and prohibit botting/automation. |

## Relevance to PartyCare

PartyCare currently provides a manual visual panel only. It is not listed on the approved addons page. Therefore, no build of PartyCare—visual-only or click-cast—should be described as permitted for HorizonXI use until its source is publicly hosted and HorizonXI staff explicitly approve it.

A player-initiated click differs from an autonomous action, but the policy also makes approval status an independent requirement. Before adding click-cast behavior, obtain a written staff ruling for PartyCare’s exact public source and interaction model.

## Boundary Maintained in This Package

PartyCare continues to exclude target selection, spell-command dispatch, input injection, packet operations, and automated decision/action behavior.
